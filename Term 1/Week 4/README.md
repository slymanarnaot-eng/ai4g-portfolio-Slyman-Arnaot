**Project title:**
The Silent Garden
**My pair partner:**
Yassir Balah
**Tool we had to use:**
ComfyUI
**SDG we had to address:**
SDG 13 - Climate Action
**What problem does it solve, and for whom?**
Our project focuses on the decline of pollinators such as bees and butterflies. Climate change and extreme weather are some of the pressures that can affect pollinators and the ecosystems they are part of.
Our main users are young adults aged 18–30, especially university students and young people who use social media. They may already know about climate change but may not think much about how it affects pollinators and nature.
**What did you build?**
We built The Silent Garden, a short AI-generated climate awareness film made in ComfyUI.
The film shows a healthy garden slowly changing as the heat increases. The flowers disappear, the bees stop coming, and the garden becomes silent. At the end, one flower is still alive with the message: “When nature becomes silent... we should listen.”
Our goal was not to create another solution for saving bees, but to make this complex environmental problem easier to understand and more emotionally meaningful in around 30 seconds.

**How do I run it?**
The final film can be watched directly on YouTube.
For the project files, open the ComfyUI workflow files in the hackathon/ folder. The workflows show how we generated the images, video clips, narration, and final video.
**Who did what?**
Me: I worked on the research, the concept and story of the film, the visual prompts, generating the images and video in ComfyUI, the narration, and putting the final video together.
My partner: He assisted with brainstorming, helped with the desk research, and prepared the slides.
We worked together on the overall idea and discussed the direction of the film.

**Ethical reflection - what are the risks of your tool? Who could it harm?**
Because we used AI-generated visuals, there is a risk that people could think the scenes are real. We used a fictional garden so we are not presenting a fake real-world event as if it happened. We also tried not to suggest that climate change is the only reason pollinators are declining, because it is one of several pressures. The silent garden is a metaphor and not a prediction of what every ecosystem will look like. Another issue is the energy used by AI generation, so we kept track of our generations and considered whether the energy use was justified by the educational purpose of the project.

## 4. Reflection

**What is the most important thing I learned this week?**
The most important thing I learned was how much planning is needed when using AI to create a complete project. It is not just about generating an image or video. We had to think about the story, prompts, consistency between scenes, movement, narration, timing and the final message.
I also learned that AI-generated content can be useful for communication, but we still need to think about whether what we create could mislead people.
**Where does this connect to "AI for Good"?**
Our project connects to AI for Good because we used AI as a tool for environmental awareness rather than just creating something for entertainment. Existing solutions already focus on things like habitat restoration, reducing pesticides and monitoring pollinators. Our contribution was different: we focused on communication and awareness, using AI to make the issue understandable and emotionally meaningful for a young audience.
