## Project Details

* **Pair Partner:** Ahmet Ünlü (24125822)
* **Tool Used:** Lovable / Bolt.new
* **SDG Addressed:** SDG 4 — Quality Education

## Problem & Solution
Helps parents with limited time and resources prepare young children for school entry by providing structured, interactive early-learning and skill-building modules.

## Ethical Reflection
* **Who is excluded?** Children without access to digital devices, high-speed internet, or environments lacking adult supervision.
* **Assumptions:** Assumes basic screen interaction capability (tap/click), access to audio output, and minimal adult guidance.
* **Potential Misuse:** Over-reliance on digital screens replacing real-world tactile learning and personal social interactions.
